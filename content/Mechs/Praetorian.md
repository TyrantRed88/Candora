## Overview

**PRAETORIAN** is [[Arcus|Arcus Blackwater's]] personal combat frame: broad, black-armored, and built around physical authority.

Its battlefield identity centers on decisive presence rather than delicacy. PRAETORIAN carries a sword, shoulder-mounted artillery, and the hardlight **Shield of Dawn**, translating Arcus's ethos into machinery: responsibility is concentrated, and the machine physically arrives where that responsibility must be borne.

## Combat Doctrine

PRAETORIAN is designed to make commitment visible.

It moves toward points of pressure, establishes a protected center, and forces hostile action to account for its presence. Protection and aggression are consequently intertwined rather than separate functions: holding ground, drawing pressure, creating space for allies, and answering threats directly are all expressions of the same battlefield role.

## Known Systems

### Primary Melee Weapon

A large sword serving as PRAETORIAN's principal close-combat weapon.

### Shoulder Artillery

Integrated shoulder-mounted heavy weaponry provides ranged firepower without compromising the frame's primary close-combat identity.

### Shield of Dawn

A hardlight defensive system used to intercept and withstand incoming force.
