# Overview

Arkady IV is the fourth planet orbiting the Arkady Star in the Meridian System. It is the only broadly habitable planet in the system and serves as a popular destination for both spacefarers and tourists alike. The planet is officially a free planet under its own rule, but it is effectively in the possession of the Consortium's House Everiss due to its staggering debt to the merchant legacy for their support in repairing and reactivating the Fargate that occupies a space between the orbit of Arkady IV's triple lunar system.
