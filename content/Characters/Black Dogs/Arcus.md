### DISTINCTIONS

#### **King Presumptive** *(d8)*
*Arcus knows that, one day, he will win his throne. While this vision drives all that he is, this Distinction represents when that drive is challenged or held out of his reach by an immediate tangible obstacle.*
#### **Soldier of Fortune** *(d8)*
*Arcus is both a captain of mercenaries, a man who lives by his own path, and someone chosen by divinity. This Distinction represents him evoking these existential truths.*
#### **One Banner, Many People** *(d8)*
*Arcus believes that there is no one people who should unite beneath his banner, for there is no one quite like him. He is surprisingly willing to take former enemies, dangerous criminals, and even species viewed with mistrust or called monstrous to his side so long as they are willing to work with him towards his goal.*
##### *No Order Given, I Wouldn't Follow (Effect)*:
When "One Banner, Many People" supplies the Effect die, create or step up an appropriate asset representing orders, coordination, morale, or an opening for an ally, using the Effect die's rating.
