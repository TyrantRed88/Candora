### DISTINCTIONS
#### **I Was Raised Better Than This** *(d8)*
*Daphne was raised from birth to become the head of the Blackwater family, learning composure, etiquette, social maneuvering, and the responsibilities of leadership. She knows how to read a room, navigate power dynamics, and carry herself with confidence when appearances matter. It represents the lessons and expectations of her upbringing—both the ones she values and the ones she’s trying to leave behind.*

#### **I Keep My Promises** *(d8)*
*Daphne takes her word seriously; once she commits to something, she will see it through even when it becomes difficult or inconvenient. It represents her loyalty, dependability, and determination when someone is counting on her. To Daphne, a promise isn't something you make lightly—it's a responsibility you choose to carry.*

##### *Then You’re With Me (Technique)*:
*Spend 1 PP when Daphne sincerely promises to protect or help someone with whom she has no Bond. Create a temporary d6 Bond with that character for the rest of the scene.*

#### **Full of Wanderlust** *(d8)*
*Daphne has spent most of her life dreaming of a freedom she was never allowed to have, and now she intends to experience it. It represents her curiosity, adventurous spirit, and desire to explore unfamiliar places, people, and experiences. When something catches her interest, she has a hard time simply walking away.*

##### *I’ve Never Done This Before (Effect)*: 
*When "Full of Wanderlust" supplies the Effect Die on an action Daphne has genuinely never attempted before, create a Beginner’s Luck d6 Advantage.*
